About Me

I design and document electro-mechanical systems for aerospace applications at Collins Aerospace, applying ASME standards, GD&T, and PDM configuration management in Agile environments. My work supports critical avionics testing and integration programs. Alongside my professional role, I am studying Computer Science full-time, gaining expertise in software development, AI, and embedded systems. This unique combination allows me to approach engineering challenges from a systems-level perspective, blending hardware design with software solutions. I also mentor high school robotics teams, sharing knowledge in CAD, sensor integration, and embedded programming.
